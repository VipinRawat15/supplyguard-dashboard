# SLA Risk Response Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the standard operating response for shipments based
on their SupplyGuard-predicted late-delivery risk level. All operations
staff should use these thresholds when deciding how urgently to act on a
flagged shipment.

## Risk Level Definitions and Required Response

### Very High Risk (predicted probability >= 75%)
Shipments in this band require same-day review by the operations team.
Assign a case owner within 2 business hours of the order entering this
band. Case owner must confirm current shipment status with the carrier
and determine whether an upgrade to a faster shipping mode or an
alternate carrier is feasible before the promised delivery window
closes. If no corrective action is feasible, proactively notify the
customer per the Customer Communication Policy before the delivery
window is breached, not after.

### High Risk (predicted probability 55-74%)
Shipments in this band should be reviewed within one business day.
Case owner should verify carrier tracking status and confirm the
shipment is progressing normally. No customer notification is required
at this stage unless tracking shows an active delay; if a delay is
confirmed, escalate to Very High Risk handling immediately.

### Moderate Risk (predicted probability 35-54%)
No individual case review is required. Moderate-risk shipments should be
included in the daily automated monitoring report and re-scored as new
tracking information becomes available. If a shipment remains in this
band with no tracking updates for more than 48 hours, escalate to High
Risk handling regardless of the model's original score.

### Low Risk (predicted probability < 35%)
No action required. Standard automated tracking applies.

## Escalation Ownership
The on-shift logistics coordinator owns all Very High and High risk
cases. Moderate and Low risk shipments are monitored by the automated
dashboard alerting system with no dedicated human owner unless they are
re-escalated per the rules above.

## Review Cadence
The full risk-ranked shipment worklist should be regenerated at the
start of each business day using the most recent order and tracking
data, and again at midday for orders placed that morning.
