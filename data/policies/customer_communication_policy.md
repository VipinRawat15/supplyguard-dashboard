# Customer Communication Policy

## Purpose
Defines when and how customers should be proactively contacted about a
shipment flagged as at-risk of late delivery.

## When to Notify
- **Very High risk, no corrective action feasible**: notify the customer
  proactively at least 24 hours before the original promised delivery
  date, or immediately if the promised date is less than 24 hours away.
  Never let a customer discover a missed delivery window without prior
  warning if the risk was known in advance.
- **High risk with confirmed active delay** (per carrier tracking):
  notify within 4 hours of the delay being confirmed.
- **Moderate or Low risk**: no proactive notification. Standard
  order-confirmation and shipping-confirmation emails are sufficient.

## What to Communicate
Proactive at-risk notifications should:
1. Acknowledge the specific order and shipping method chosen.
2. State plainly that the delivery may arrive later than originally
   promised, with a revised estimated window if available.
3. Offer a concrete remedy where policy allows — e.g., a shipping-fee
   refund for expedited modes that fail to meet their promise, or a
   small courtesy credit for repeat/high-value customers.
4. Avoid over-promising a new date unless carrier tracking data
   genuinely supports it; a second missed promise is more damaging to
   trust than one clearly-communicated delay.

## Channel
Use the customer's original order confirmation channel (email is the
default). Do not introduce a new communication channel for at-risk
notifications, as this increases the chance the message is missed or
flagged as spam.

## Segment Consideration
Corporate and Home Office segment customers, who more frequently place
recurring or bulk orders, should be prioritized for proactive outreach
over one-off Consumer segment orders when operational capacity for
manual outreach is limited, since a repeated delay pattern with a
recurring business customer carries higher relationship risk.

## Do Not Notify Cases
Do not send at-risk notifications for orders with `Order Status` of
CANCELED or SUSPECTED_FRAUD — these orders are not proceeding to
shipment and a delivery-delay notification would be confusing and
inappropriate. Fraud-suspected orders should instead be routed to the
fraud review team per the Fraud and Cancellation Handling Policy.
